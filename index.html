Backlot Havoc is a free-to-play portrait action game for Android and iPhone, previously called Blockbuster Battles. Follow a 3D campaign road through 90 levels across Superhero, Fantasy, Horror, Western, Arctic, Adventure, Dino, Sci-Fi and Mythic arenas. Fight through connected rooms, enemy waves and survival encounters.

Drag to dodge, release to fire with ranged heroes, and build combinations from your power choices. The campaign begins with five easier tutorial waves and a mini-boss. Later stages have 20 rounds, mini-bosses on rounds 5, 10 and 15, and a final boss on round 20.

Collect 14 characters, upgrade cards and powers, equip gear and bring a pet companion. New arenas expand pack rewards. Optional prize events unlock as you clear the opening campaign levels.
