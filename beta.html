Play Backlot Havoc on iPhone or iPad through Apple TestFlight.

Request received

When your invitation is ready, Apple will send it to the address you entered.

  1. On your iPhone or iPad, install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open Apple’s invitation email on that device and tap View in TestFlight.
  3. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install next to Backlot Havoc.
  4. Tap Open once it installs. You can send feedback or check for updates in TestFlight.

If the invitation hasn’t arrived, check your spam folder. For help, contact support.
